# Privacy

Clipshot collects nothing.

- It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- It reads images only to find the screenshot that was just taken. It ignores everything else.
- It keeps a private copy of the last three screenshots, so the clipboard keeps working. They never leave the app's private storage, except through the clipboard when you paste.
- It stores four settings and counters on the phone (on/off, remove from gallery, the last copied screenshot, how many were copied). They are excluded from backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
